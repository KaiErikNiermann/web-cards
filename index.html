Card material study

Hover to tilt. Drag a card through its row and the others make room. Double-click (double-tap) sends it home.

sway: rotateZ = −k · vx, spring-damped
reflow: slot = nearest home of dragged centre
push: falloff (1 − d/R)² outside own row

Material

Finish

Snap off: cards land where dropped and every nearby card is pushed away instead. Roughness widens and dims the highlight lobe; reflectance scales it and the edge clearcoat; foil is a spectral band (color-dodge) whose phase follows view angle.

Faces: SVG-cards 4.0.2 by David Bellot and Huub de Beer, LGPL-2.1, rasterised at 846 × 1224. Art: The Battle for Wesnoth (Rise of Wesnoth story art, core story art “Wesmere”), GPL-2.0-or-later, 1920–2560 px sources.
